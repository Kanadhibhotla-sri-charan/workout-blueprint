## Record rules

- Every entry answers “when would I choose this?”
- A variation is separate only when it changes loading, path, stability, constraint, tolerance, or programming role.
- Plain language precedes technical detail.
- Scores guide decisions; they never diagnose or guarantee results.

## Taxonomy

### Upper body

- Chest: clavicular/upper, sternal/mid, costal/lower.
- Back: lats, mid-back, rhomboids, upper/mid/lower trapezius, spinal erectors.
- Shoulders: anterior, lateral, posterior deltoid; rotator cuff; serratus anterior.
- Arms: biceps (long head, short head), brachialis, brachioradialis, triceps (long head, lateral head, medial head).
- Forearms and grip: wrist flexors, wrist extensors, pronation, supination, grip/fingers.
- Neck: flexors, extensors, lateral flexors.

### Trunk and hip complex

- Core: rectus abdominis, obliques, transverse abdominis, quadratus lumborum.
- Hip complex: gluteus maximus, gluteus medius/minimus, hip flexors, adductors, abductors, tensor fasciae latae.

### Lower body

- Quads: rectus femoris, vastus lateralis, vastus medialis, vastus intermedius.
- Hamstrings: biceps femoris, semitendinosus, semimembranosus.
- Lower leg: gastrocnemius (medial/inner head, lateral/outer head), soleus, tibialis anterior.

## Movement Taxonomy

- Horizontal press and horizontal pull
- Vertical press and vertical pull
- Shoulder abduction, flexion, extension, and rotation
- Elbow flexion and extension
- Wrist flexion, extension, pronation, supination, and grip
- Squat / knee-dominant
- Hinge / hip-dominant
- Hip extension, flexion, abduction, adduction, and rotation
- Knee flexion and extension
- Ankle plantarflexion and dorsiflexion
- Trunk flexion, extension, rotation, lateral flexion, and anti-movement

## Review Gate

Before an entry becomes Reviewed, it must have a complete schema, a clear purpose, realistic limitations, a meaningful alternative or complement where relevant, and evidence notes for material claims.
