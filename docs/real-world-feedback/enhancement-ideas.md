# Enhancement Ideas

Ideas that are not current defects — things Blueprint doesn't do yet, rather than things it does wrong. Keeping these separate from [`bugs.md`](bugs.md) prevents every new idea from becoming an immediate engineering task; per [`docs/architecture/DEPLOYMENT-AND-PRODUCTION-READINESS.md`](../architecture/DEPLOYMENT-AND-PRODUCTION-READINESS.md) §19, the architecture is frozen unless a real requirement cannot be represented cleanly by the existing model.

Example from the spec:

```text
"I wish I could save favorite exercises."
```

A product feature, not a recommendation-engine defect — belongs here, not in `bugs.md`.
